# Privacy Policy for PageSnap

*Last updated: October 23, 2026*

PageSnap is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed with a **privacy-first, zero-telemetry architecture**.

---

## 1. No Data Collection or Transmission
- **PageSnap does not collect, track, log, or sell any personal information or browsing history.**
- All content extraction, Readability processing, and Markdown conversion run **100% locally** in your browser tab.
- No network requests are made to any remote analytics, tracking, or telemetry servers.

---

## 2. Explanation of Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `activeTab` | Grants temporary access to read the DOM of the active tab only when you click the extension icon or trigger a conversion. It does not allow background monitoring of other tabs. |
| `clipboardWrite` | Allows copying the generated Markdown directly to your system clipboard when you click "Copy". |
| `storage` | Stores your local user preferences (e.g. YAML frontmatter on/off, Obsidian wikilinks preference) inside your browser. |
| `downloads` | Allows saving exported `.md` and `.html` files to your computer's Downloads folder upon your request. |
| `contextMenus` | Provides the right-click shortcut "Copy region as Markdown" for quick extraction. |

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
PageSnap does not share data with any third-party services, advertisers, or AI training pipelines.
